Our Initiative

Building skills. Creating opportunity. Empowering lives.

OMGIIT is committed to quality computer education, digital skills and employment-oriented training, with an emphasis on inclusive learning and the empowerment of Persons with Disabilities.

As part of our social responsibility and inclusive education initiative, we are working towards the skill development and empowerment of Persons with Disabilities by providing accessible computer training and opportunities to become self-reliant.

We kindly request individuals, institutions and organizations to extend financial support towards this meaningful initiative.
